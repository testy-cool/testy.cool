Every arrow and label on this page is the library running live. No screenshots,
no JavaScript.

[neat-annotations](https://github.com/syabro/neat-annotations) is one CSS file,
8.5 KB, free to use, by Maxim Syabro. You wrap a word in a tag, give it a class
name and a `data-note` with the label text, and it draws this:

<AnnStage>
  <Ann note="the target" dir="n">a word</Ann>
</AnnStage>

The class name is a compass direction. It says where the arrow points, so
`ann-n` sends the arrow up and parks the label underneath.

<AnnStage>
  <div className="grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-8 gap-y-40 md:grid-cols-4">
    <span><Ann note="ann-n" dir="n">n</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-ne" dir="ne">ne</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-e" dir="e">e</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-se" dir="se">se</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-s" dir="s">s</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-sw" dir="sw">sw</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-w" dir="w">w</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="ann-nw" dir="nw">nw</Ann></span>
  </div>
</AnnStage>

Six colours, and the highlighter mark can be turned off.

<AnnStage>
  <div className="grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-8 gap-y-24 md:grid-cols-4">
    <span><Ann note="amber" dir="s" color="amber">one</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="blue" dir="s" color="blue">two</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="green" dir="s" color="green">three</Ann></span>
    <span><Ann note="no mark" dir="s" noMark>four</Ann></span>
  </div>
</AnnStage>

Flip the theme with the switch at the top right. Every colour has a light and a
dark version.

## Three things to know

**The label takes up no space.** This paragraph has a marked word in it, and the
label for <Ann note="down here" dir="n" color="red">this one</Ann> lands on
the next line, on top of the text. The page lays out its lines as if the label
were not there. That is why every other example on this page sits inside a
panel with room around it.

**A marked phrase is one solid box.** When it wraps onto a second line, the
arrow points at the whole block, not at a word.

<AnnStage>
  <p className="mx-auto max-w-56">
    <Ann note="one box, two lines" dir="n" color="purple">
      a phrase this long wraps and the arrow loses its target
    </Ann>
  </p>
</AnnStage>

**The handwriting font is yours to supply.** The labels expect a font called
Shantell Sans. Without it you get whatever the computer calls its cursive font,
on the right.

<AnnStage>
  <div className="grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-8 gap-y-24">
    <span><Ann note="Shantell Sans" dir="n">with the font</Ann></span>
    <span>
      <Ann note="your cursive" dir="n" style={{ "--ann-font": "cursive" }}>
        without it
      </Ann>
    </span>
  </div>
</AnnStage>
